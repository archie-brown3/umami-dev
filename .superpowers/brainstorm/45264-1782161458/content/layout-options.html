README Header Layout

Which arrangement of the three app screenshots feels right for the header?

A — Equal Triptych

Three phones side by side at the same size. Clean, balanced, symmetric — easy to scan at a glance. Works great for dark or gradient backgrounds.

B — Hero Center

Center screen is larger (the recipe detail view), flanked by two smaller screens. Draws attention to your best-looking screen while showing breadth.

C — Staggered Cascade

Phones rotated and overlapping in a fan/cascade. Dynamic and energetic — feels more like a marketing banner than a documentation screenshot.
